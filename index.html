Infrared receiver board for a robot

Robotics team project
Goal
Give our robot a sensor that could detect and tell apart red and green infrared beacons.
What I built
I assembled and soldered the board, installing more than eight component types including resistors, capacitors, transistors, ICs, IR detectors, LEDs, and a fuse, while checking values and polarity.
What went wrong
The finished board didn't work. I ran power and continuity tests, found weak solder joints, and reworked them one at a time.
Result
My team and I validated a working backup board, and the robot completed its beacon-detection and retrieval tasks.
  • Soldering
  • Circuit assembly
  • Continuity testing
  • Debugging
